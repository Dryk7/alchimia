You are a runner lost in the wasteland. No one is watching. Not yet.

Tap the screen to make your hero run. Jump over hurdles at the right moment. Pass rivals. And watch the world change around you: from the dusty construction site to the suburbs, then to urban avenues, all the way to the gold of the Olympic cauldron.

FOULÉE is not your generic idle clicker with numbers climbing in a vacuum. Every km you cover **actually** transforms the scenery: 5 unique biomes, stands that fill with crowds, floodlights igniting, and a final sprint you will never forget.

- 🌍 **100% offline, no forced ads** — Play in airplane mode, in the subway, anywhere
